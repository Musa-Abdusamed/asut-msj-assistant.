# ASTU MSJ Bootcamp

The bootcamp is designed to help students improve their programming and problem-solving skills.

# Registration Requirements

Students must provide their full name, email address, department, academic year, GitHub username, and Codeforces username.

# Assignments

Students are expected to solve programming problems every week. Assignment submissions must be completed before the weekly deadline.

# Attendance

Students are expected to attend bootcamp sessions regularly. Students should communicate with their mentor if they cannot attend a session.

# Programming Practice

Students should practice programming problems on platforms such as Codeforces. Regular practice is important for improving competitive programming skills.

# Mentors

Students can contact their mentor when they need help with programming practice or assignments.
